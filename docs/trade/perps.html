WHAT THIS IS
A composite of 5 perp market structure metrics, each scored –1 to +1. Equal-weighted average. Threshold ±0.30 for directional read.

WHAT IT IS NOT
This is not an institutional signal. It cannot distinguish institutional from retail flow, hedging from speculation, or accumulation from distribution. Each metric has hard limits on what it can reveal — read the tooltips.

OI: Net contract creation/destruction only. Direction and composition unknown.
FUNDING: Cost of holding leveraged long via perp. High funding supports basis trades, not just crowding.
L/S RATIO: Retail-skewed exchange data. Large player positioning not captured.
CVD: Aggressive order flow direction only. Passive counterparty composition unknown — could be absorption.
VWAP PROXY: OI × volume estimate. No true price data on free tier. Direction indicative, magnitude approximate.
